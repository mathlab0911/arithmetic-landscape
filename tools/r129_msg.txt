Terminology tables in both editions, and a seam pass after the surgery

Each live paper now carries a terminology table: eleven rows in Part I glossing
the vocabulary of the series, three and five local ones in Parts II and III.
Each row says what the term denotes in ordinary language, and Part I's closing
sentence says which of the eleven are the point and which are scaffolding -- two
and nine.  The Japanese editions of Parts I and II carry the same tables.

The coset identity's row now also fixes the sense of "coarse-graining", which was
being used three times in Part III as a bare adjective while the identity itself
had been renamed: the family {X(v.)} is closed under coarse-graining exactly, and
the phrase is used below in that sense and no other.

coherence_r129 is a seam pass.  Ten commits in two days touched every paper --
the definition of Gamma, the symbol for the representation count, three
abstracts, the terminology tables, the assembly of Part III.  Each edit was
checked; the seams between them were not.  It probes the specific ways those
edits leave a paper contradicting itself: a surviving \deg, a surviving
"order-sensitive", two names for the window measure, lm defined the old way, an
abstract promising a symbol that no longer exists.  Seven hits, six of them the
deliberate retirement notes, one real -- the coarse-graining adjective, fixed
above.
