Three checks that read what the other twelve could not see

C13.  Every check was scoped to paper/.  The Japanese editions had never been read by
any of them, and the constant erratum corrected in English at r118 was still in
paper2_ja four times, through two months of green runs.  The new check is narrow --
a decimal literal printed in a translation must occur in its source, because
translations restate numbers rather than compute them.  Negative control taken.
Widening C11 to paper-ja found a fifth literal, exempted in the English and not in the
Japanese though it is the same sentence; exemptions now cross the language boundary,
since an exemption is a fact about the number.

C14.  The definition change at r120 left paper 3 defining Gamma by the retired
enumeration series twice and paper 4 once, with paper 2 and its translation still
calling it "an order-sensitive invariant" -- which the layer definition makes simply
false.  Nothing numeric was wrong, so none of the numeric checks could see any of it.
C14 is a population lock in the spirit of C4's label snapshot: count the sites where
Gamma appears beside a dyadic sum over the elements and pin the count -- 5 in paper 1,
5 in its translation, 0 elsewhere -- taken after collapsing whitespace so it does not
move when a paragraph reflows.

And then C14 passed while this README's own headline still defined Gamma by the
enumeration series and called it order-sensitive.  Same mistake as C13, within the
hour, immediately after writing the ledger entry about it.  The scope of a check gets
chosen while thinking about the rule, when the artefacts are not in mind.  So: every
check now prints what it looked at, and C9 verifies the README's claim about how many
checks exist against the list that actually runs.

C15.  A reference to another paper's numbered result is a string LaTeX cannot resolve.
Paper 2 said "answers Problem 10.1 of the companion paper"; inserting the extremal
section into paper 1 renumbered it to 11.1 and nothing noticed.  Paper 3's "in the
style of paper 1 S10 and paper 2 S10" had been wrong since it was written -- those are
the CV section and the main theorem, not the two status ledgers it meant.  The papers
now write \Xref{stem}{label}{number} and \Xlab{stem}{label}, resolved against the
sibling's .aux with the printed number required to match.  All nine resolve.

The consolidation inventory behind C15 is xref_r120: 90 prose references to a sibling
paper (paper 2: 14, paper 3: 34, paper 4: 42), 6 label references, 3 numeric.  Paper 1
has none, which is the right shape for the root.  Also recorded and not fixed: papers 3
and 4 declare bibliography entries P1, P2, P3 that are never cited, because every
sibling reference is prose.

README: the headline now states the layer definition and the extremal bounds; the check
table reaches C15; fifteen checks, and C9 now checks that number too.
