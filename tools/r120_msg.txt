A scale that was wrong as printed, three lemmas before the two we had flagged

Paper 2's honest-scope section names steps 2 and 4 of the deep-minor-arc
proposition as its debt.  Auditing them found the debt smaller than advertised --
both are written out -- and a defect next door, in the two lemmas that hand theta
to them.

Two variables live on that circle, the angle theta and the normalised theta/2pi,
and the argument moved between them without saying so.

- lem:cover was stated for |theta - 2pi j/q| <= 1/(q tau), while its proof --
  Dirichlet applied to alpha_n = n theta / 2pi -- produces a bound on
  |theta/2pi - j/q|.  As stated the lemma did not follow from its proof.
- lem:annulus wrote theta = 2pi j/q + t and then split
  S_B(alpha_n) = sum_r e(n r j/q) sum_{p = r (q)} e(p n t).  That split is the one
  for theta = 2pi (j/q + t); lem:twist, which it invokes, takes t on the
  normalised scale.

Checked rather than argued: scale_r120 evaluates both sides of the residue split
over the primes below 200 at q=6, j=1, n=3, t=0.0137.  Under 2pi(j/q+t) the sides
agree to 1.4e-13; under 2pi j/q + t they differ by 8.9.  One reading is an
identity, the other is not.

Both lemmas are now on the normalised scale.  The covering lemma's reduction is
written out -- |theta/2pi - j/q| <= 1/(Q tau) <= 1/(q tau) since q divides Q -- so
it now loses no constant at all.  Nothing downstream moves: the 2pi is absorbed by
the O() and by the e^{-c sqrt(log N)} saving, which is why it survived.  Recorded
in sec:remains as a found-and-closed item, in both editions.

The point is not the factor.  It is that the defect was three lemmas away from the
two steps the status ledger had marked as risky.  A ledger records where you know
you are weak; defects prefer the places you have stopped looking.
