Stopped before the assembly: a DROP row would have deleted a proved lemma

The paper-3 mapping table said DROP lem:kappa, "the multiplicative
kappa-transport, refuted at r-graveyard; Part III does not use it".

lem:kappa is the ADDITIVE BRIDGE.  It is titled "the bridge", it is proved with
its proof following the statement, and it is cited nine times in paper 3 -- by
the introduction, by region R3 twice, by prop:tiltlclt's own status ("R2 and R3
proved (they quote Lemmas lem:kappa and lem:eta)"), by the bridge section's
summary, and by the honest-scope list as "Proved: the bridge -- Lemma lem:kappa
in full".  What is refuted is the MULTIPLICATIVE transport |G~| <= |G|^kappa,
which is rem:noKappa's subject and the graveyard's entry.  Two objects sharing a
substring in their names.

Dropping it would have removed a lemma three results depend on, and because the
assembly moves text rather than compiling it, the loss would not have raised a
build error -- it would have surfaced later as a proposition quoting a lemma
that is not in the paper.

The row survived both my table and fable-5's independent audit.  I wrote it by
hand from the graveyard memory, matching on "kappa".  The audit checked the row
against my stated reason, and the reason matched the same memory the reason came
from -- and about the multiplicative bridge that reason is entirely correct.
Both passes verified conformance; neither put the row next to the paper.  An
independent auditor reading the same description is not an independent check of
the description.

DROP_GUARD: a DROP whose subject is still referenced anywhere refuses to
classify and demands an account of where the references go.  lem:kappa: nine
references, refused.  Negative control taken.  The asymmetry that puts the guard
here rather than on additions: additions announce themselves at build time,
deletions do not, so a deletion is the row that must be read against the primary
source.

Corrected table: MOVE 21 (was 20), CALIB 8, OPEN 2, SPLIT 1, DROP 1 (was 2),
still 33 environments each appearing once.  The sole DROP is rem:noKappa, kept
under fable's requirement (B) with an explicit account of its single reference.

The assembly has not been started.  It waits on two questions put to fable in
