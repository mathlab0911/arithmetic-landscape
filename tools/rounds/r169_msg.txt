DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21941261, and the badge that carries its caveat with it

The repository is deposited in Zenodo and has a DOI. The concept DOI above always resolves to
the latest version; release v1.0.0 (commit ad4964c) has its own, 10.5281/zenodo.21941262. The
record is registered with DataCite, carries the author's ORCID, and is now the single work on
that ORCID record.

The README badge is deliberately not just a badge. Underneath it sits one line saying the
manuscripts are CC BY 4.0, the code Apache-2.0, and -- in bold -- that none of this is
peer-reviewed and every statement carries its own status where it is stated. A DOI looks like
an imprimatur to a reader who has not thought about it, and this one is not: it certifies that
a version exists and is permanent, not that it is right. The caveat travels with the badge
because the badge is what people copy.

The same sentence is in the Zenodo description, under its own heading, as the third of four
paragraphs -- after what the mathematics says and what is in the deposit, before the tool and
computational resource disclosure. Anyone who reads far enough to cite this will have read it.
