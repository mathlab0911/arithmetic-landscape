The algorithmic reading is the one worth attention. It attached two conditions to the sentence
about restart counts. One was ours and is gone; the other is not ours and never will be, so it
now stands alone and loud -- the uniformity of the terminal distribution is an assumption about
the search, not a fact about the landscape. Closing a gap makes the remaining gap more
prominent, not less: with one caveat where there were two, a reader stops discounting the list
and starts reading it.

sounds like verification and is not is worse than no procedure.
