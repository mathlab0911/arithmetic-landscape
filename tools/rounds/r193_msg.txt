governance.md: the three-layer model mapped honestly, two provisions adopted, two declined, one
priced

Kentaro commissioned the mapping and fable-5 designed it; references/governance.md writes it up.
The identification is real -- C1-C20 plus the status vocabulary plus the report protocol are a
deterministic orchestrator, the referee pass is a non-participating auditor, the head/hands
cross-verification is mutual evaluation -- and the document spends its length on the two places
where it fails instead. Our orchestrator is deterministic about form and silent about sense,
which is the hole the referee pass exists to cover after eps*(Z) = eps*(Z) passed all twenty
checks. Our auditor is invoked rather than standing, which is cheaper and weaker, and is stated
rather than glossed.

Adopted: A-1, the evidence-path declaration -- every verification names the artefacts it touched
and declares independence from the author's description of them, as a required report field. It
mechanises the lem:kappa lesson (approving a description is not approving the thing) and it makes
the r175 defect impossible to write: three incompatible descriptions of one independent reading
could not survive a field that asks which text each reading covered.

Adopted: A-2, the status-transition table -- each edge names its mover and its evidence class,
with two deliberate asymmetries. Raising a status requires the other party; lowering does not.
Every edge is reversible and every reversal is recorded, which is why prob:R1 stays in the paper
restated as CLOSED.

Declined: JSON scoring, because a numeric score is a currency and a currency invites optimisation
of the currency -- the currency here is a named artefact and a named falsifier, which can be
spent by someone who distrusts us. Declined: role rotation, because the split is a division of
instruments and rotating it would rotate away the asymmetry that makes correction possible.
Priced rather than declined: continuous auditing, at roughly one fresh-context session per round.

And recorded in the ledger: fable-5's r192 makes the skill APPLY the first item of the next
session, before any mathematics. It has waited five rounds. This round did the commissions that
fit and did not start the approved mathematics, and it did not do the APPLY either, for the same
measured reason as before. A standing order deferred with a reason is still deferred, and the
count is now in the ledger where it can be read against the excuse.

C1-C20 pass.
