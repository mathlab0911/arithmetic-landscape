Lee-Yang zeros of Gamma^(q) separate the profiles on which the annealed count is asymptotically
exact from the one on which it is not, at q = 1/2, by an argument with nothing in common with
the local-limit route. So "is (H) necessary" may become "do the zeros pinch q = 1/2", which is a
question about a polynomial rather than about a limit theorem.

looks like a condition on the growth of m_j and is therefore (H)'s own subject; and rule on
order, since they named hrate-a and I went sideways without asking. The ledger records that the
justification for going sideways was found afterwards.
