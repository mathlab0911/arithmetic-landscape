The ledger folded: nineteen entries archived, the distillation written exactly

Rounds r160-r180 are in ledger_archive.md, unedited, under a header naming what the period
covers: the lift of prob:R1 and the three theorems becoming unconditional, the Leiden signature
and the ORCID, the first DOI and the v1.1.0 release, the first referee pass, and door 2.
ledger_pending.md is empty again, with the standing rule restated at its head -- write the case
the hour it happens in the words that were true at the time, and distil only when folding,
because the distillation is a different act from the record and doing both at once produces
neither.

tools/skill_delta_r181.md is the distillation, written so it can be applied verbatim:

  F78  the dangerous failure of a checker is the empty scan, not the false alarm; and a check
       repaired after a rename must accept the old names, because one that recognises only the
       current wording cannot audit the past.
  F79  anything that says "pending X" needs a person who will notice when X happens, because the
       file will not -- twice in one day, the second time hours after writing the rule about the
       first. The release procedure now asks: what did this release make false?
  F80  a fresh reader is not a more careful version of the author; it is a different instrument,
       and it measures something the author has no access to. All three referee-pass findings
       were claims about our own evidence rather than about the mathematics.
  F81  an identifier looks like an imprimatur; put the caveat where the claim gets copied. A DOI
       makes a version permanent; it does not make it true.
  F82  the second identical attempt is diagnosis, the fourth is denial; and when an interface has
       a cache, a green screen is not evidence -- ask the layer underneath.

Plus strengthenings: F26/F27 gains "a plateau is a claim about a range, and three points inside
one are not a range" and the rule that when two people disagree by a factor the first suspect is
the weight; F35 gains fable's constitutional ruling that a reader who cannot see the old status
cannot audit the new one; F38 gains "a status is a label on a statement, and a label cannot fix a
verb"; F60 gains the rename as its seventh instance and the observation that writing a claim out
for someone else is a check, and the only unmechanised one we have; F70 gains "when a correction
makes a bound uglier, the ugliness is information about where the old bound was borrowing".

And three for the division of labour: design cannot check itself against magnitudes it has not
computed; execute faithfully and report back when the execution refuses; the scope of an
authorisation is bounded by what the person could plausibly have been imagining when they gave
it.

SKILL.md itself is NOT rewritten in this commit, and r181 in the ledger says why. save_skill
replaces the file whole, so updating it means reproducing 55 KB by hand, and a silent
transcription error there would corrupt the one document this project uses to remember why it
does things -- invisibly, because that file has no checker, no build, and nothing that would
fail. The instrument that records how we avoid mistakes is itself unchecked. So the write was
split: the permanent record made safe first, the delta written exactly, and the unverifiable
write left for a session with room to read it back and diff it. That is F82 applied to ourselves
rather than to a button.
