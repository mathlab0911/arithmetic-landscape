The window: fable-5's control law confirmed, the discriminating measurement unresolved, and a
fitted agreement that was noise

fable-5's r186 derived rho_k = Q_k/sigma^2 ~ (c/2)^k and released the window experiment with the
discriminator stated: the layer mechanism gives an exponential decay whose rate moves with c as
log(2/c); the zeros give a k-independent constant and cannot.

The derivation checks out line by line, and it was checked before use, as they asked. Measured,
rho_k*(2/c)^k is flat at c = 1.6 (0.8646 to 0.8542 over k = 12..28), converging at c = 1.4, and
NOT settled at c = 1.8 by k = 28 -- so the law is confirmed where it is confirmed and the claim
is not extended past that. The specified positive control passes: Q(0) at c = 1 runs 20.0744,
20.2973, 20.3289, 20.3328 toward 61/3.

Then the signal, and the part worth the round. The first run took four k per c and fitted decay
rates 0.390885 and 0.237397 against log(2/1.4) = 0.356675 and log(2/1.6) = 0.223144 -- ratios
1.0959 and 1.0639. That is the prediction confirmed to within ten per cent, and the sentence
saying so was written.

Pushing to every reachable k -- nine points at c = 1.4, six at c = 1.6 -- the same fits give
0.1141 and 0.0262, with a residual spread of 3.2 in log e. Over a k-range of 8 that is an
uncertainty of about +-0.4, larger than either predicted value. The agreement was an artefact of
stopping at four points: no bug and no wrong formula, but a fit over a range too short to see its
own scatter, quoted as a ratio to four significant figures. The decimals are what made it
persuasive.

This is F27 firing on its author one round after that author wrote "a plateau is a claim about a
range, and three points inside one are not a range" into the ledger. The second time a rule
catches you is not a failure of the rule; it is the rule working on someone who already believed
it.

Why it cannot resolve, in one column: at c = 1.6 the relative error e/Gamma is still O(1) at
every k for which lm/r is exactly computable -- 0.275, 0.105, 0.763, 0.028, 0.182, 0.161 -- so
the limit has not begun and there is no rate to measure. The enumeration was improved this round
(B_d takes only k distinct values as d runs, so k subset-sum tables suffice instead of a_k/2 of
them) and it bought about six values of k. Six is not enough.

Registered: control law confirmed on c in {1.4, 1.6}; the discriminating measurement does not
resolve; the pre-registered falsifier did not fire, and that is not evidence either way, because
an experiment that cannot resolve cannot exonerate. fable's Reading-2 conjecture is neither
supported nor damaged.

Two clauses handed back for ruling rather than asserted: print the residual spread beside every
fitted rate; and establish the reachable range before fitting, not after.

And the gap named rather than filled: everything is exactly computable at large k except lm/r,
and lm/r is the only one of these quantities that touches the landscape rather than the
generating function. There is no candidate replacement observable, and saying so is better than
substituting the next computable thing.
