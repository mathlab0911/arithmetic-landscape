Door 2 opened: the Lee-Yang zeros of Gamma^(q), and they separate the profiles

spec_future_r145 listed this as door 2, priced at an afternoon, with three outcomes written
down in advance. It produced all three at once, on different profiles.

Gamma^(q) is a polynomial in q for finite A. Grouping layers by their count gives
Gamma^(q) = 1 + sum_j m_j [q^j + (1-q)^j] with m_j = (a_{j+1}-a_j)/2, degree k-1, symmetric
under q -> 1-q, and equal to Gamma(A) at q = 1/2 up to O(2^-k).

For the odd numbers every m_j is 1 and the two geometric sums collapse:
q(1-q)Gamma^(q) = 1 - q + q^2 - q^{k+1} - (1-q)^{k+1}. Substituting q = w/(k+1) sends this to
1 - e^{-w}, whose zeros are 2*pi*i*n, so Im q_1 = 2*pi/k + O(k^-2). The zeros DO pinch the real
segment, but only at its endpoints q = 0 and q = 1 -- the two degenerate measures -- and the
fair coin is left alone: the nearest zero to q = 1/2 sits at distance 0.5021 at k = 96 and is
going to 1/2, not to 0. Measured k*d runs 6.0518 to 6.2784 against 2*pi = 6.283185, from below,
like 1/k. Primes and random odd sets give the same picture.

Then the same computation on a_i = 2^i + 1, the lacunary witness for which the annealed
prediction fails. There m_j = 2^{j-1} exactly, so Gamma^(q) = 3 + (1/2) sum [(2q)^j +
(2(1-q))^j] -- two geometric series whose ratios are complex conjugates of equal modulus
precisely on Re q = 1/2, where they can cancel. They do. Re q_1 = 1/2 to every digit computed
and Im q_1 = 3*pi/(2k): measured k*Im runs 4.7736, 4.7457, 4.7299, 4.7214, 4.7169 at
k = 128..2048 against 3*pi/2 = 4.712389, and |q_1 - 1/2| runs 0.0520, 0.0253, 0.0167 and is
going to zero.

So the Lee-Yang zeros separate the profiles on which the annealed count is asymptotically exact
from the one on which it is not, and they separate them at q = 1/2 -- the value at which all of
Parts I and II is stated. Two routes with no argument in common, the local-limit route of
sec:bridge and the zero-counting route here, draw the line between the same families.
