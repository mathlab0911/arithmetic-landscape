F113 -- COMPRESSION LOSES FACTORS, AND THE COMPRESSED FORM IS THE ONE THE READER GETS.  "the sine series ... its limit is 2 zeta(s)" was false as written; the series tends to zero and the quantity with that limit is the series divided by t.  The 1/t is present and correct in the written proof and was lost in the compression to prose.  The repair rule: a summary is a NEW claim and must be re-derived, not checked against its source -- checking it against the source verifies the summary against a description of itself (F52), because the eye supplies the missing factor from the neighbouring display.  Second clause: an all-red pass is a measurement of SCOPE, not of quality; the number to report is not "nine defects" but "nine defects in the region no check looks at".
