# How to finish the r200 fold — one step, from a session with room

**Do this first, and let it have the turn to itself.** It needs room to read ~80 KB and emit
~80 KB. That is r194's rule, bought over six deferrals: *a cost measured only against turns that
already had work in them is not a measurement of the cost.*

1. Read `tools/skill_backup_r200/SKILL_after_r200.md` in two halves — `sed -n '1,540p'` and
   `sed -n '541,1075p'` via bash, **not** `Read`, whose line-number prefixes would have to be
   stripped by hand.
2. Call `save_skill` with `name: "pnp-research"`, `overwrite: true`, the description **exactly**
   as it appears in that file's YAML front matter, and `content` = the file's body (everything
   after the closing `---` and its blank line; `save_skill` rebuilds the front matter from
   `name` and `description`, which is why the r194 save came back byte-identical).
3. **Verify before believing it.** `md5sum` the skill's cached `SKILL.md` against
   `SKILL_after_r200.md`. Expected: **80197 bytes, 1075 lines, md5
   `14b6321770e633b4e8f2fdc051216124`**, and `F01`–`F87` all present
   (`grep -o '\*\*F[0-9][0-9]\*\*' | sort -u | wc -l` = 87).
4. If the diff is not empty, re-save from the file. If it is empty, **empty
   `tools/ledger_pending.md` back to its header** — that is the step whose omission would leave
   two canons again — and say in the report that C6 now reports zero explicitly.

**No grep spot-checks beside the hash.** One of them printed nothing at r194 while its subject
was present, defeated by a line break at column 96. Where a hash is available a grep adds
nothing but a way to be wrong.
